# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Be direct, be kind, and argue about evidence rather than people.

Examples of expected behaviour:

- Critique a claim, method, or figure without attacking the person who made it.
- State uncertainty and cite the source you are relying on.
- Respect different levels of domain knowledge; explain acronyms.
- Accept correction gracefully and correct the record when you are wrong.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualised language.
- Personal attacks, sustained disruption, or bad-faith editing.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Misrepresenting a source, a result, or another contributor's position.

## Scope

This code applies in repository spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when representing the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a concern privately through the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** channel, or contact a maintainer directly. Do not open a public issue that contains personal or sensitive information.

Maintainers will review reports in good faith, keep them confidential where possible, and choose a response proportionate to the behaviour: a private warning, a request to edit or withdraw content, temporary limits on participation, or a permanent ban. Maintainers who are involved in an incident will not decide the outcome alone.
